# Advanced mode

Only use this intake when the user explicitly chooses Advanced. Ask the questions together with their explanations, using the exact wording below. Answers can be brief, partial, or unknown. Do not turn the explanations into additional required questions.

1. **Who is the project primarily intended for?**
   The people the page should speak to most directly.
2. **What knowledge or experience do you expect those people to have?**
   This helps the audit judge whether explanations are appropriately technical or assume too much.
3. **What is the main thing the project enables them to do?**
   Its core purpose, before getting into supporting features.
4. **What do they gain from using it?**
   The practical benefits: less work, better results, more control, creative possibilities, or something else.
5. **Which of those benefits should be the strongest reason to try it?**
   This establishes what deserves emphasis, rather than treating every benefit as equally important.

Form a page-only interpretation before consulting these answers. Then compare the intended audience with the audience addressed, the intended purpose with the function explained, and the benefit priority with the emphasis on the page. Report specific gaps; do not assume author assertions prove product capabilities.

Default Audit has no intake. Missing or ambiguous positioning is evidence to report, not a reason to withhold the audit.
